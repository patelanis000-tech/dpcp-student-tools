IBDP · November 2026Examination confidentiality

Examination content must remain confidential.

This requirement continues after you have left the examination room.

24HOURS

after each
examination ends

Do not share outside your immediate examination cohort.

For 24 hours, you must not discuss or share questions, topics, answers or hints with anyone outside the group of students at this school who sat that examination with you. This includes friends at other schools, tutors and family members.

Do not publish examination content online.

Do not post it on social media, forums or public groups. Do not send it to anyone outside that examination cohort, including through a group chat. A private message still counts as sharing.

The two-hour supervision period does not replace the 24-hour confidentiality restriction. Being dismissed does not end this restriction.
Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures02 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Before the examination

Arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled start.

Wear your regular school uniform and bring the materials you need for the correct paper.

01

Report to the examination venue.

Check your examination date, start time, subject and level in the timetable. Report to the venue 15 minutes early so checks can be completed before the examination begins.

02

Store your belongings.

Switch off your phone and disable all alarms. Leave bags, watches and other personal belongings in the designated storage area identified by the invigilator. Do not keep prohibited items in your pockets.

03

Enter and sit as instructed.

Enter quietly when invited and sit at your assigned desk. Your identity must be verified. Remain silent and do not open the examination paper until instructed.

A school ID card is not compulsory when staff can otherwise verify your identity. Bring proof of identity if the coordinator has asked you to do so.
Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures03 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Permitted materials

Take only authorised materials to your desk.

All items must be available for inspection. Permission for one paper does not apply to another.

Bring the necessary stationery.

Bring dark blue or black non-gel pens, a soft dark pencil, a ruler, an eraser and any geometry instruments required. Bring enough stationery: you must not borrow or share during the examination.

Use a fully transparent pencil case.

All contents must be clearly visible. Opaque or partly opaque cases are not permitted. Keep only authorised stationery inside the case.

Bring water in a clear, label-free bottle.

Your bottle must be transparent, with all labels removed, and must contain water only. It must not carry notes or other unauthorised material.

Check paper-specific permissions.

Calculators and bilingual dictionaries may be taken to the desk only when permitted for that paper and approved beforehand. A lucky charm requires inspection and approval before it may stay on your desk.

Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures04 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Prohibited materials

Keep prohibited items away from your desk.

You must not have them on your desk, in your pockets or within reach during the examination.

Phones, watches and personal clocks

No phone or wristwatch is allowed at the desk, even if switched off. This includes ordinary watches. Do not bring an individual desk clock; use the examination-room clock.

Headphones and other electronic devices

Do not take headphones, earbuds, tablets, laptops or other unauthorised electronic devices to the desk. Personal earplugs are allowed only under an approved individual arrangement.

Notes, books and unauthorised paper

Leave bags, study notes, books and loose paper in the designated belongings area. Only the materials authorised for your particular examination may be used.

Personal tissue packets and other items

The school provides tissues; raise your hand to ask for them. Keep personal tissue packets with your belongings. Food and drinks other than water require a medical exception.

Possession of unauthorised material is a breach even if you do not use it. Declare any unauthorised items before the examination starts.
Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures05 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Calculator procedure

Give your GDC to Mr. Anis before examination day.

Your graphical display calculator must be approved and correctly configured for the examination.

01

Before examination day

Hand your GDC to Mr. Anis. He will inspect and approve it, then set it up in the required examination mode. Do not wait until you are entering the examination room to arrange this.

02

Bring it to each permitted examination.

Bring the same approved GDC, charged and ready, to each examination in which calculators are allowed. Do not disable examination mode or change the restricted settings. Report any loss of the mode to Mr. Anis.

03

It will be checked every time.

Before each examination, Mr. Anis or an invigilator will check that your GDC is still in the correct examination mode. Staff will reset or reconfigure it where required. Advance approval does not replace this check.

After each examination: follow staff instructions for required memory clearing. No calculator may be used during reading time or in a non-calculator paper.
Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures06 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Translation dictionaries

A bilingual dictionary requires advance approval.

Do not bring a dictionary to your desk unless Mr. Anis has approved both your eligibility and that copy.

Check your eligibility first.

A printed bilingual translation dictionary may be permitted when the examination’s response language is not your strongest language. Taking English B does not, by itself, establish permission.

Submit the dictionary before examination day.

Give your dictionary to Mr. Anis for inspection and approval before examination day. The approved copy must remain free from notes, annotations and added material.

Use it only in permitted examinations.

Do not use a bilingual translation dictionary in Language A or language acquisition examinations, including English B. Any specific Classical Languages allowance must be confirmed separately.

Follow the restrictions during use.

Electronic dictionaries are not permitted. Do not share a dictionary or consult it during reading time. Approval for a different paper is not permission for the paper you are sitting.

Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures07 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Writing and answer materials

Use the correct pen, pencil and answer space.

Your answers are scanned for marking. Write clearly and follow the instructions for each part of the paper.

Written answers: dark blue or black ink

Use a non-gel pen for written responses. Use a soft pencil that produces dark lines for graphs and diagrams. Coloured pencils are permitted only in Geography examinations.

Multiple-choice answers: pencil

Complete multiple-choice answer sheets in pencil, following the printed instructions. This does not mean that written-response sections in the same paper should also be answered in pencil.

Corrections and highlighting

Cross out mistakes neatly. Do not use correction fluid or correction pens. After reading time, you may highlight questions, but not your answers.

Working and additional space

Use the examination paper or provided answer booklets for working and plans. Do not use separate scratch paper. If you need more space, raise your hand and continue in an answer booklet; identify the question and indicate the continuation in the answer box.

Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures08 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Reading time

Reading time is for reading only.

Most examinations include five minutes of reading time before the writing time begins.

05:00

Do not begin until
the invigilator tells you.

Wait for permission to open the paper.

Check the subject, level and other details as instructed. Only open the examination paper when the invigilator gives permission.

Do not write, mark or use equipment.

During reading time, do not write answers, make notes, highlight questions, use a calculator or consult a dictionary. Keep pens and pencils down.

Start writing only on the instruction.

You must wait until the invigilator announces the writing time. The five minutes of reading time are not part of the stated writing time.

Stand-alone multiple-choice papers do not have this five-minute reading period. Follow the instructions for your specific paper.
Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures09 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Conduct in the room

Examination conditions apply as soon as you enter.

Follow every instruction from the invigilator and remain silent until you have left the examination room.

Stay in your assigned seat.

Do not change seats, stand up or leave the room without permission. Raise your hand and wait for an invigilator to come to you.

Do not communicate with other candidates.

No talking, signalling, passing items, helping another candidate or receiving help. Do not share stationery, calculators, dictionaries or other equipment.

Keep your work independent.

Do not look at another candidate’s answers or make your work available for copying. Unauthorised notes and devices are prohibited, whether or not you intend to use them.

Ask about procedures, not answers.

Raise your hand if a page is missing, you need an answer booklet or something is wrong. Invigilators cannot explain a question, interpret it for you or help you answer it.

Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures10 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Temporary absence and support

Ask permission before leaving your desk.

Necessary bathroom visits, sudden illness and approved arrangements have specific procedures.

01

Bathroom visits

Raise your hand and wait for permission. A member of staff must supervise your temporary absence. Do not take materials out, access your phone or notes, or speak to anyone except the supervising staff member.

02

Illness and medication

Tell an invigilator immediately if you feel unwell. Arrange necessary medication and individual access needs with Mr. Anis beforehand where possible. Do not leave the room on your own.

03

Tissues and approved exceptions

Raise your hand to request school-provided tissues. Personal earplugs, food and drinks other than water are not general permissions; any individual arrangement must be agreed in advance.

A supervised bathroom visit or medical absence is not early submission or dismissal. Do not assume the time away will be added to your examination time.
Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures11 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Supervision and departure

You may leave only when an invigilator permits it.

Finishing your work does not end the required supervision or give you permission to collect your phone.

A minimum of two hours applies.

Remain at your assigned desk until the required supervision ends, even if your paper is shorter than two hours. The two hours run from the examination start, not from when you finish.

Early departure has restrictions.

For a longer sitting, you may ask to leave after two hours only if you have finished all required papers. No early departures during the final 30 minutes of the relevant examination.

Consecutive papers remain supervised.

Stay supervised between papers and do not collect your belongings. No early departure during the first hour of a paper. Follow the exact sequence and release instructions for your sitting.

Permission and supervision still apply.

Wait for explicit permission. Staff must confirm that the required supervision is complete before releasing you. You cannot return to a paper after handing it in and leaving early.

Two hours is not automatic release. Approved bathroom and medical absences follow the separate temporary-absence procedure.
Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures12 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Lateness, illness and emergencies

Report lateness, illness or an emergency immediately.

Do not make your own arrangements to miss, delay or reschedule an examination.

If you are running late

Contact Mr. Anis or the school promptly and report to the venue as soon as possible. Explain what has happened. Do not assume a late message gives you permission to arrive at a different time.

If you are unwell before the examination

Tell Mr. Anis or the school as soon as you can so the appropriate procedure can be followed. Do not assume that illness automatically excuses an absence or that you can sit the paper on another day.

If a problem occurs during the examination

Raise your hand and tell the invigilator immediately. Do not wait until you have left the room to report a problem that is affecting your ability to complete the paper.

If the room must be evacuated

Follow the invigilator’s instructions and remain under supervision. Do not talk about the paper or access your phone. Staff will tell you what to do next.

Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures13 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Ending the examination

Stop writing immediately when instructed.

Do not add a final word, finish a calculation or make any other change to your answers after time is called.

01

Put your pen down.

Stop all work on your answers. Follow any subsequent instructions about checking identification details and organising your script. These instructions do not permit further work on answers.

02

Check and secure your script.

As directed, check your name, session number and question numbers. Attach the required coversheet, answer booklets and graph paper in the correct order. Ask if you are unsure.

03

Hand over every examination material.

Leave examination papers, answer materials, data booklets and unused paper for collection. Do not take any examination material out of the room. Complete the required calculator checks.

04

Remain silent until dismissed.

Stay seated while materials are collected and counted. Leave quietly only when permitted. The 24-hour restriction on sharing examination content continues after dismissal.

Sunway International School · Examination rules and procedures14 / 15
IBDP · November 2026Examination confidentiality

Do not share or obtain confidential examination content.

These restrictions apply to conversations, private messages, group chats and public posts.

01

Do not describe the paper to outsiders.

For 24 hours after it ends, do not send questions, topics, answers, screenshots or hints to anyone outside your immediate examination cohort. Reconstructing a question from memory is still sharing examination content.

02

Do not seek or circulate leaked material.

Never search for, request, buy, download or distribute unreleased examination questions or answers. A private chat or invitation-only group is not an exception. Do not help someone who has not yet sat the examination.

03

Report anything you receive.

If you receive material claiming to contain examination questions, do not continue reading, use it or forward it. Tell Mr. Anis immediately and follow his instructions for reporting the concern.

Suspected examination misconduct is reported to the IB. Penalties can include no grade for the affected subject and no diploma.
